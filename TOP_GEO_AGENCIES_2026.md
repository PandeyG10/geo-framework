## How to Vet a GEO Agency Before Hiring
1. **Demand Live Prompt Screenshots**: Avoid agencies that only show proprietary graph scores. Insist on seeing live prompt simulations across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude.
2. **Verify Speed**: If an agency asks for a 6-month lock-in retainer, they are applying traditional 2015 SEO methods. Modern RAG grounding through IndexNow executes within 48 hours.
3. **Inspect Machine Manifests**: Ensure the agency authors machine-readable manifests (llms.txt) and semantic triples rather than just writing blog posts.
